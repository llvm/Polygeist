# Larger MFEM application extraction and raising results

## Summary

- Applications represented: 7
- Concrete operator entries: 11
- Frontend successes: 11/11
- Raising-pipeline successes: 11/11
- Entries with no residual loops: 10/11
- Raised Linalg operations: 834
- Semantic matcher groups: 167
- Emitted candidate `kernel.launch` operations: 167

These counts describe concrete hot operator applications, not whole MPI
programs. Mesh handling, restriction/prolongation, communication, and solver
control remain in MFEM.

## Per-entry results

- `mtop_iso_elasticity`: complete 2D dfem hot path; 76 Linalg operations,
  zero loops, 16 cuTensorNet contraction candidates.
- `dfem_minimal_surface`: complete padded-scalar 2D hot path; 34 Linalg
  operations, two residual loops in the nonlinear `sqrt` quadrature function,
  and 8 cuTensorNet contraction candidates in interpolation/integration.
- `ex35p` H1: complete diffusion + mass branch; 94 Linalg operations, zero
  loops, 19 structural GEMM candidates.
- `ex35p` H(curl): curl-curl portion; 146 Linalg operations, zero loops, 29
  structural GEMM candidates. `VectorFEMass` is not yet extracted.
- `ex35p` H(div): div-div portion; 66 Linalg operations, zero loops, 12
  structural GEMM candidates. `VectorFEMass` is not yet extracted.
- `ex9p`: mass + convection element-batch path; 42 Linalg operations, zero
  loops, 9 contraction/AXPBY candidates. The iterative mass solve remains
  application orchestration.
- `grad_div`: div-div portion; 66 Linalg operations, zero loops, 12 structural
  GEMM candidates. `VectorFEMass` is not yet extracted.
- `abs_l1_jacobi`: complete mass and diffusion branches plus the curl-curl
  portion; respectively 24, 70, and 146 Linalg operations, all with zero
  loops. The curl branch still lacks `VectorFEMass`.
- `navier_tgv`: complete scalar pressure-diffusion operator; 70 Linalg
  operations, zero loops, 14 structural GEMM candidates. The vector mass,
  vector diffusion, nonlinear convection, discrete divergence, and discrete
  gradient families remain to be extracted.

## Interpretation

The matcher results are semantic candidates. They do not by themselves prove
that emitting every candidate as an independent vendor-library launch is
legal or profitable. In particular, the structural `cublasGemmFor1x1Conv`
matches need shape/stride ABI validation, and application performance will
require grouping or fusing adjacent recognized stages.

The next coverage work is therefore well-defined: add MFEM's
`VectorFEMass`, `VectorDiffusion`, vector/nonlinear convection, discrete
gradient/divergence, and a pointwise-Linalg representation for the
minimal-surface `sqrt` quadrature function.
